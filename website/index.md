Native macOS apps

### Pick the tool for the job.

Browse a photo folder, make a video easier to share, or find a moment in an old clip. Your files stay on your Mac.

Video storyboard

#### ShotMap

Find the right video without playing every file.

Turn key moments into a single storyboard image. Let Smart Select get you started, then keep or replace any frame on the timeline.

- Batch processing

- Manual frame editor

- PNG / JPG export

macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel

[Download](https://github.com/ixiehao/ShotMap/releases/latest) [Website](https://ixiehao.github.io/ShotMap/) [Source](https://github.com/ixiehao/ShotMap)

Image viewer

#### LumaView

Open an image. Browse the whole folder.

Move between images, zoom into details, check EXIF, or read a PDF. No importing files or building a photo library.

- Folder browsing

- EXIF details

- RAW & PDF preview

macOS 12+ · Apple silicon & Intel

[Download](https://github.com/ixiehao/LumaView/releases/latest) [Website](https://ixiehao.github.io/LumaView/en/) [Source](https://github.com/ixiehao/LumaView)

Video converter

#### MP4Flow

Convert videos to MP4. Keep the parts you need.

Convert a batch, trim a clip, crop the frame, rotate, or merge videos. Install FFmpeg and ffprobe separately before your first conversion.

- Batch conversion

- Trim · crop · rotate

- Merge clips

macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel

[Download](https://github.com/ixiehao/MP4Flow/releases/latest) [Website](https://ixiehao.github.io/MP4Flow/en/) [Source](https://github.com/ixiehao/MP4Flow)

All three apps include English, 简体中文, Français, Русский, Español, 日本語, and 한국어. Downloads and installation instructions are on GitHub Releases.
