How I work

The software should earn its place.

My work is shaped by a simple belief: a good tool respects attention, files, and the platform it lives on.

  1. 01

    Native first

    Software should feel at home on the platform it serves.

  2. 02

    Local by default

    Personal files should stay on your device whenever possible.

  3. 03

    Useful over busy

    A small tool should do one important job well.

  4. 04

    Open by design

    Good tools improve when their implementation is inspectable and shared.
